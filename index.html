Our Love Story Timeline

Flip through the real chapters of our journey (2016 – Present)

2016

The Beginning of Our Journey

  • January 11, 2016: Our beautiful love story officially began in Madhya Pradesh.
  • We spent quality time together in the school classroom, where our love first blossomed.
"Where our story officially started in Madhya Pradesh..."
2017

10th Class, The Public Proposal & Family Support

  • Classroom Memories: We were placed in different sections for 10th class, but we looked forward to the evenings during classroom sweeping duties just to meet and talk.
  • Birthday Surprise: Even after completing my exams, I went back to school just to celebrate her birthday with her while she was still giving her exams.
  • The Grand Proposal: Before finishing 10th class, right around the final exams, I proposed to her in front of the entire classroom.
  • First Family Conversation: During final exams in 2017, I opened up to my father about our relationship. He was understanding and told me that once we both got settled in our futures, our families would happily accept our bond.
  • Summer 2017: During the summer break, Facebook Messenger was our only source of contact, and we spent hours messaging each other.
"A fearless leap of love and our very first promises."
2017 – 2018

Intermediate / First Long-Distance Phase & Secret Hostel Call

  • First LDR Phase: I joined Intermediate 1st year, marking our first long-distance chapter. We eagerly waited for holidays just so we could call and text each other.
  • The Night Call Mystery: In 2018, we pulled off a secret operation—sneaking a mobile phone into her hostel. We stayed up all night excited, talking about our memories and sharing special moments.
  • Hostel Teasing & Secret Signals: In the days after, her friends teased her constantly, waiting for my calls. To contact her, whenever my parents or friends' parents visited, I would give her a missed call, and she would call me back so we could talk.
"Miles apart, but closer at heart with every passing night."
2019

JEE Mains, B.Tech, and First Outings

  • Early 2019 (JEE Mains Day): She surprised me by coming to my exam center on the day of my JEE Mains exam. Seeing each other brought so much excitement!
  • First B.Tech Reunion (Railway Station): When I started B.Tech in 2019, we met at the railway station. I was so nervous and shy that I couldn’t even hold her hand for the first time—a memory we fondly laugh about now!
  • First Outing (Oxygen Park, 2019): We went on our very first official outing to Oxygen Park, creating one of our cutest initial memories together.
"Shy hand-holds and unforgettable first dates."
2020

Bhongir Fort, Corona Lockdown & Virtual Love

  • Bhongir Fort Trip: We went to Bhongir, a crucial landmark in our journey that brought us significantly closer to each other.
  • Pre-Lockdown Meal: Before heading home, we ate lunch together at Bawarchi / Mehfil, assuming we would meet again in a few weeks or months.
  • Corona Lockdown LDR: Lockdown separated us unexpectedly for much longer than planned. We kept our connection alive through video calls, Instagram, Zoom meetings, and sending funny/crazy snaps on Snapchat.
"Overcoming distance through virtual smiles and endless calls."
2021

A Special Gift Across the Distance

  • January 11, 2021 (Anniversary Surprise): While she was working in Hyderabad and I was at home, I sent her a surprise gift for our anniversary: a golden rose and a special locket that projects "I Love You" in 100 different languages.
"Expressing love in a hundred different languages."
2022

Surprises & Vikarabad Trip

  • Vikarabad Trip: When her friends were going on a trip to Vikarabad, I showed up to surprise her, making another unforgettable trip together.
  • Quality Weekend Time: By October 2022, we were meeting every weekend, celebrating our birthdays together, exchanging gifts, and enjoying continuous quality time.
"Surprise road trips and precious weekend escapes."
2023 – 2024

Internship, Father’s Blessing & US Departure

  • 2023 (Internship Days): I started my internship, giving us more private, quality time together. Weekend dates became our regular ritual.
  • Late 2024 (US Visa & Dad's Approval): She received her US visa to pursue her Master’s (MS) in the United States. Before she left, I spoke to my father again about our relationship, and he fully accepted us.
"Stepping into big futures with parental blessings."
2025

Oceans Apart & Mom’s Acceptance

  • US Long-Distance Phase: 2025 was our biggest long-distance challenge yet with the massive time zone gap. Despite the difficulty, our bond only grew stronger.
  • Mom’s Blessing: During 2025, I shared our entire story with my mother, and she happily accepted our relationship as well.
"Bridging continents and oceans with unwavering trust."
2026

Reunion & Her Parents' Approval

  • Return to India: She came back to India in 2026! We spent an incredible amount of quality time together, meeting every weekend.
  • Her Parents Accept: During this phase, she spoke to her parents about our relationship, and they lovingly accepted our union. Both families are now fully supportive of our journey.
"Reunited at last, blessed by both families! ❤️"
